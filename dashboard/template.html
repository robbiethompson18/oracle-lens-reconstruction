Oracle Lens on WildChat

Qwen3.6-27B answers a WildChat prompt. At every 4th token of its reply, an oracle lens reads the residual stream at four depths and writes what the model is "thinking" as bullets. Click a highlighted token, or scrub with the slider / ← →.

Lens: the WorkspaceBench authors' public checkpoint (agu18dec/olens_and_ar, RL step 600), not ours yet. The read at a token is the residual after the model has consumed that token, i.e. while it decides the next one. Greyed text to the right of the selected token is what the model went on to write; the lens never sees it. Sampled once at T=1, so readouts are noisy by design.
